PRIVACY POLICY

Effective October 23, 2026.

The Long Cosmos upload tool is used only by the channel owner to upload, schedule and manage videos on The Long Cosmos YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's consent through Google OAuth.

The tool does not collect, sell or share personal data from viewers or other users. OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's machine and used only to manage the channel's videos. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. YouTube's own use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
